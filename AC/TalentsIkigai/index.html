Your Ikigai

Your Ikigai is the independent creation of immersive digital experiences and visual craft—building evocative, structurally sound video games and designs that champion moral courage, celebrate human decency, and solve complex technical puzzles on your own terms.

The Four Pillars of Your Ikigai

  • What You Love: The convergence of technology and artistic creation. You are energized by autonomous creative output—specifically game development and visual design—as well as suspenseful, atmospheric storytelling, principled character, and an orderly daily rhythm that balances hard physical exertion with deep creative focus.
  • What You Are Good At: Applied technical systems and visual-spatial architecture. Your cognitive profile reveals a rare blend: a 3D Visualizer and Visual Scanner paired with Sequential Thinking, Diagnostic Problem Solving, and Masterful Vocabulary. You grasp computer systems intuitively, manipulate spatial environments effortlessly, diagnose technical hitches rapidly, and ship complete products independently (exemplified by publishing a mobile game).
  • What You Can Get Paid For: Independent and contract Game Development, Graphic Design, interactive visual computing, and specialized digital production. Your interest profile (Realistic, Artistic, Conventional) points directly to work where tactile or technical execution meets creative design within structured, organized systems.
  • What the World Needs: Tangible compassion, ethical fortitude, and moral clarity. Your heart is oriented toward practical relief (feeding the hungry), honoring quiet decency (validating and encouraging kind people), upholding the Golden Rule, and standing firm for what is true even when it is costly.

Integrated Reflection & Guidance

Your YouScience profile and reflections clearly show that you are an autonomous technical builder, not a middle-manager, committee facilitator, or classroom instructor. You possess the comprehensive skill set needed to carry a project across multiple disciplines without needing an army of specialists.

How to spend your time at work:

  • Operate as an Independent Craftsman or Founder: You marked a clear preference for running "your own thing" and thrive as an Introvert with a Generalist approach. Build studio independence through indie game releases, high-leverage freelance design contracts, or specialized technical art projects.
  • Harness the "3D Visualizer + Diagnostic Problem Solver" Edge: Lean heavily into 3D environments, game engines, physics-based mechanics, and complex digital production tools. Because you diagnose flaws rapidly and track sequential processes naturally, you are exceptionally suited to building clean game logic, debugging engine pipelines, and designing tactile, satisfying mechanics.
  • Infuse Substantive Themes into Your Work: You are drawn to tension, suspense, and stories of people standing up against immense pressure to do what is right. Channel this into your games and designs. Subtle, atmospheric, and resonant narratives that honor courage, integrity, and human dignity will distinguish your portfolio.
  • Protect Solitude and Outsource Instruction: Accept your self-assessment that instructional teaching is not your calling. Protect long blocks of uninterrupted flow for building and design, and let your finished products speak on your behalf.

How to spend your time at play:

  • Anchor Your Days in Physical Discipline: Your Realistic nature and structured focus demand physical expression. Keeping a dedicated 6:00 AM gym routine, working with your hands on small construction or tactile projects, and maintaining physical vitality ground your analytical mind and clear out digital fatigue.
  • Engage in Direct, Local Community Impact: You feel pulled to address hunger and uplift kind people. Rather than getting bogged down in institutional politics, find direct, low-friction ways to serve—stocking a local food pantry, preparing meals, or performing quiet acts of generosity.
  • Curate Deep Rest and Connection: Protect shared evenings with your fiancée, screen films that challenge and entertain you, and prioritize your desire to travel abroad to experience different cultures. Stepping completely outside your domestic and digital workspace will feed your visual imagination.
