Career requirements & recommendations

A public portfolio or playable demo is more important than credentials alone. Studios evaluate source code, gameplay loops, and design documents.

  • Education: Transfer credits toward a four-year Bachelor of Science in Computer Science, Game Design, or Software Engineering.
  • Portfolio: Maintain a public GitHub repository demonstrating game mechanics.
  • Practice: Participate in game jams.
  • Tools: Master Unreal Engine 5 and Godot 4.

Matches with YouScience

Spatial Visualization

Level layout, 3D world building, camera mathematics, and spatial physics.

Numerical & Sequential Reasoning

Scripting, conditionals, state machines, vectors, and algorithms.

Artistic & Conventional

Creative aesthetics and narrative design paired with asset structure and documentation.

Introvert Style

Long, uninterrupted coding, level construction, and debugging sessions.

Possible gaps to consider

Specialization

AAA studios often seek narrow specialists. Generalists are stronger in indie development, technical design, or small teams.

Communication

Professional studios require design reviews, sprint planning, and cross-disciplinary standups.

Career requirements & recommendations

Certification and practical testing are central to the field, especially for structural and pressure-pipe work.

  • Certification: Pursue AWS or ASME structural and pressure-pipe test plates.
  • Safety: Complete an OSHA 10-Hour or 30-Hour card.
  • Practice: Complete practical structural and pipe welding test bends.
  • Experience: Gain hands-on fabrication skills through internships or machine shops.

Matches with YouScience

Realistic

Hands-on tool operation, material manipulation, and physical fabrication.

Conventional

Weld codes, dimensional tolerances, and welding procedure specifications.

Spatial Visualization

Blueprint schematics and cut lists become multi-axis metal assemblies.

Numerical & Sequential Reasoning

Joint geometry, heat input, feed speeds, and weld passes.

Introvert Style

Focused, solitary bench and booth work behind the hood.

Possible gaps to consider

Creative expression

Standard welding offers limited artistic variety. Custom fabrication, ornamental ironwork, or automotive prototyping can help.

Work variety

Welding often rewards repetitive procedural mastery. General fabrication and repair shops may provide broader task variety.

Planning Summary

Game Developer Path

Best suited to a person who enjoys creative problem-solving, spatial thinking, programming, and long uninterrupted development sessions. The strongest portfolio is a public, playable game with version-controlled source code and documented gameplay.

Welder Backup Path

Best suited to a person who enjoys physical fabrication, precise procedures, safety, and focused solo work. The strongest preparation is hands-on experience, recognized certifications, and practical welding qualification testing.
