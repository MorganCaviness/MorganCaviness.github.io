About Me

Morgan Caviness

I am a 25-year-old man from Burleson, Texas. I attended Hill College in Burleson for General Studies. In 2020, I moved to San Marcos, Texas where I attended Austin Community College before progressing to Texas State University for a degree in Communication Design with a focus in Graphic Design. After one year at Texas State, school became too expensive—unwilling to take on the debt—I dropped out. Not for nothing, during this time I met my now-fiancée, Mary-Claire. In August 2026, we moved to Amarillo, her hometown.

I enjoy graphic design and video game development. I am days away from releasing my children's mobile game, Cully Bubble❐, and I'm always looking for new creative challenges and opportunities to grow both personally and professionally.

Wanting a large family, I chose to pursue a stable, future-oriented career that would allow me to provide for them. When I'm not in class, I am picking up shifts at Water Still, developing my next video game, reading my Bible, or spending quality time with Mary-Claire.
