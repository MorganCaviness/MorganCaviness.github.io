Cully Bubble

Unreal Engine 5 • Google Play • Gaming

My relaxing ASMR bubble popping mobile game, featuring a diverse selection of beautiful, immersive worlds.

The Development Process

Building Cully Bubble allowed me to heavily utilize Unreal Engine 5's visual scripting. I built the core gameplay loop entirely in Blueprints, focusing heavily on responsive touch controls and satisfying physics.

Visual Polish & VFX

To achieve the relaxing ASMR aesthetic, I relied on Niagara particle systems. By fine-tuning the particle forces and lifetime logic, I was able to create bubbles that feel tactile and rewarding to pop.

About Me

I am a game developer and graphic designer with a passion for bringing ideas to life, both on screen and in the real world. While I spend much of my time building immersive experiences in Unreal Engine 5 or designing clean branding and assets in Affinity and Adobe, my love for creating extends far beyond the digital space.

I genuinely enjoy carpentry, building, and fixing physical items. Understanding how structures are built from the ground up constantly informs my digital design logic. To continue expanding my hands-on skill set, I am heading to Amarillo College this August to begin an advanced certificate in welding.

Whether I am visual scripting a complex game mechanic, laying out typography, or fabricating with raw materials, I am driven by the process of problem-solving and high-quality creation.
