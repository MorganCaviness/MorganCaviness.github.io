Privacy Policy

Cully Bubble
Last Updated: July 28, 2026

Welcome to Cully Bubble! This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download, install, and play our mobile game.

Your privacy and the safety of all players—especially younger users—is paramount. Cully Bubble is designed to be a safe, offline-friendly mobile experience that does not collect personal data.

1. Information Collection and Use

Cully Bubble does not collect, store, transmit, or share any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical location, phone number, or device identifiers.

All game progress, scores, and settings are saved locally on your personal device and are never uploaded to remote servers managed by the developer.

2. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Families Compliance)

Cully Bubble is designed to be enjoyed by players of all ages, including children under the age of 13. We strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and app store family standards:

  • We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 (or any users).
  • We do not require account creation or personal login details.
  • We do not feature online chat features, user profiles, or social media sharing hooks.

3. Advertising and In-App Purchases

Cully Bubble contains no third-party advertisements and no behavioral tracking SDKs. You will not be tracked across apps or websites, nor will you see targeted ads while playing.

4. Third-Party Services & Platform Services

The game is distributed through official app market platforms (such as the Google Play Store or Apple App Store). These platforms may independently collect anonymous usage telemetry or crash logs according to their own privacy policies and your individual device privacy settings. The developer does not harvest or process this data independently.

5. Data Security

Because Cully Bubble does not transmit or collect personal data over the internet, your personal information is not exposed to online server risks or data breaches through this application.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally to reflect future updates or legal compliance changes. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or Cully Bubble, please reach out via the contact form on the main website:
